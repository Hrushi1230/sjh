# 01 — Product Intent

## Experience statement
The visitor should feel as if a pair of ceremonial carved doors opens into Puri. The motion is a brand ritual, not decoration.

## Emotional sequence
1. **Anticipation** — darkness and closed architecture.
2. **Warmth** — a controlled golden seam.
3. **Arrival** — the temple is revealed as the doors physically move away.
4. **Clarity** — typography appears only after the scene has settled.
5. **Action** — one clear planning control appears last.

## What makes it premium
- strong photography + architecture, not many UI cards;
- one memorable motion idea;
- deliberate pauses;
- typography with generous rhythm;
- no visual noise after the intro;
- the CTA feels integrated into the scene.

## Scope
This handoff covers the first mobile viewport only. Destination portal/swipe and the next homepage section are phase 2.
